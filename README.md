# India CPI Inflation Analysis

## Overview

An Excel-based analysis of India's Consumer Price Index (CPI) to understand inflation trends, category-level price movements, food inflation, the impact of COVID-19, and the relationship between crude oil prices and inflation.

The project uses All-India CPI data to transform raw index data into structured analysis and business insights using Microsoft Excel.

## Business Objective

The objective of this analysis is to understand:

- How India's CPI inflation changed over time
- Which categories contributed most to overall price levels
- How food inflation changed month over month
- How inflation behaved during the COVID-19 period
- How crude oil price movements related to major CPI components
- Differences between rural and urban price indices

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- Excel formulas
- Data cleaning and transformation
- Pivot tables
- Conditional logic
- Data visualization
- Trend analysis

## Analysis Covered

### 1. CPI Category Contribution Analysis

Analyzed CPI categories and grouped individual components into broader categories to understand their relative contribution to overall price levels.

### 2. Year-on-Year Inflation Trend

Analyzed the All-India CPI General Index from 2017–2023 to identify changes in inflation over time.

### 3. Food Inflation Analysis

Examined food inflation over a 12-month period and analyzed monthly movements across food categories.

### 4. COVID-19 Inflation Analysis

Compared CPI inflation across pre-COVID, COVID, and post-COVID periods to understand changes in inflation patterns.

### 5. Crude Oil & Inflation Analysis

Analyzed monthly crude oil price movements alongside major CPI components including fuel, transport, food, and overall CPI.

## Key Questions

- What was the overall CPI inflation trend?
- Which categories had the highest index levels?
- How did food inflation change month over month?
- How did inflation patterns change during COVID-19?
- Which CPI components showed stronger movement alongside crude oil prices?
- How did rural and urban CPI indices differ?

## Key Insights

The final insights from the analysis are documented after analyzing the underlying CPI and oil-price data.

> Note: The analysis is exploratory and identifies relationships and trends in the available data. Observed relationships should not automatically be interpreted as causal effects.
